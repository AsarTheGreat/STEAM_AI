- The concept of zero was invented in India and revolutionized arithmetic.
- The Fibonacci sequence appears in flowers, shells, and spiral galaxies.
- Prime numbers are the building blocks of cryptography and secure online communication.
- A mathematical proof shows why a statement is always true, no matter the case.
- Probability and statistics help us understand risk, experiments, and real-world data.
- Algebra uses letters and symbols to solve puzzles with unknown numbers.
- Geometry studies shapes, angles, and space to help build houses, bridges, and art.
- Calculus looks at change and motion, and engineers use it to design faster cars and safer planes.
- Number theory explores patterns in numbers and helps make codes that protect information.
- Logic and set theory help computers think clearly and organize information.
- Trigonometry uses triangles to measure heights, map the Earth, and send signals.
- Math is used in music, sports, medicine, and computer games to solve problems every day.
