Something mysterious has happened at the Brightstone Museum!

Early this morning, the staff discovered that the Ancient Star Artifact had fallen out of its display case at 3:15 PM yesterday. The crash was heard, but no one saw what happened. The museum director has called on YOU, the Junior Detective Team, to solve this case!

Your mission is to ask questions and follow the clues to figure out what really happened to the artifact.

Here's what we know so far:
- The artifact was safe at 3:00 PM during a school field trip.
- A loud crash happened at 3:15 PM in the Ancient History Hall.
- Someone or something knocked it over.
- Strange evidence was found at the scene.

To begin your investigation, start with this question:

    What happened at 3:15 PM?

This will give you the first clue. Then keep asking questions based on what you discover. Look for connections between the timeline, evidence, suspects, and locations.

Ask about things like:
- Clues and evidence
- Paw prints
- Suspicious suspects
- Timeline details
- What animals could be involved

Follow the trail of clues, and you'll discover the truth!

Good luck, Junior Detective. The museum is counting on you.
